# One-Pan Chicken Thighs with Harvest Grains & Sweet Peppers

**Serves 2–3**

## Ingredients

- 4 chicken thighs, cut into pieces
- 1 cup Trader Joe’s Harvest Grains Blend, dry
- 1½ cups chicken broth
- 1–1½ cups mini sweet peppers, sliced
- ½ medium onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
- ½ teaspoon black pepper
- ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano
- ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley or sliced scallions, optional

## Instructions

1. **Season the chicken.** Pat the chicken dry and season with salt, pepper, smoked paprika, oregano, and garlic powder.

2. **Brown the chicken.** Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook for about 3–4 minutes per side, until nicely browned. The chicken does not need to be completely cooked through. Transfer to a plate.

3. **Cook the vegetables.** Reduce the heat to medium. Add the onion and mini sweet peppers. Cook for 4–5 minutes, until they begin to soften. Add the garlic and cook for another 30 seconds.

4. **Toast the Harvest Grains.** Add 1 cup Trader Joe’s Harvest Grains Blend directly to the skillet. Stir with the vegetables for about 1 minute.

5. **Add the broth.** Pour in 1½ cups chicken broth, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom of the skillet. Bring to a gentle simmer.

6. **Finish with the chicken.** Return the chicken and any accumulated juices to the skillet. Cover, reduce the heat to medium-low, and simmer for approximately 10–12 minutes, stirring the grains once or twice. Cook until the Harvest Grains are tender and the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F.

7. **Adjust the consistency.** If the grains absorb the broth before they are tender, add another ¼ cup broth or water. If there is excess liquid when they are done, uncover and simmer for another minute or two.

8. **Finish.** Stir in the lemon juice. Taste and adjust the salt and pepper. Sprinkle with parsley or scallions, if desired.

## Notes

Other vegetables that work well in this recipe include spinach, mushrooms, zucchini, frozen peas, or chopped broccoli.

Spinach should be stirred in during the last 1–2 minutes of cooking so it just wilts.
